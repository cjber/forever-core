Work down the cards. Each one says what to set up, what to do and what you should see. Mark it Pass, Fail or Skip; on a fail, say what you saw and paste a screenshot (Ctrl+V). Every answer saves as you give it.

Keys: J/K next and previous card, P pass, F fail, S skip, N write a note, Enter open or close a passed card, Esc leave the note.

Before you finish, type /reload in game once so the error log is written; finishing reads it.
