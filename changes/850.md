### Added

- **The Forever tracker column sits under the quest tracker.** By default the column now starts on the last line of the game's quest list and follows it as quests are accepted, completed or collapsed, without redrawing its sections. When the column no longer fits between that line and the bottom of the screen, it sits left of the quest tracker instead, and returns under it once there is room. Right-click any Forever section and pick **Tracker position** to keep it under or left of the quest tracker; dragging the column still places it anywhere, and a locked column holds the place it has. During combat the column holds its place and catches up when combat ends. (#850, Refs #792, #802)

### For addon authors

- `ForeverCore.TrackerHost.GetPlacement()` and `SetPlacement("under" | "left")` read and set the column's place. Setting one releases a dragged position. The retired `SetAttached` and `IsAttachedToQuestTracker` stay as no-ops, so an addon's own "Attach to quest tracker" checkbox does nothing and can be removed.
