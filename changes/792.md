### Changed

- **The Forever quest list is gone.** Your quests show in the game's own quest tracker, or in your quest addon's. No Forever addon hides, replaces or reorders the game's tracker any more. The `/fcore quests` and `/fcore profile` commands, the profile code and the Forever addons page are gone too. (#792)
- **The Forever tracker sits to the left of the game's quest tracker,** top edges level, and follows it when you move that tracker in Edit Mode. Drag it anywhere and it stays there; **Reset position** in any section's right-click menu puts it back. A position you dragged before is kept, and an older placement moves beside the game's tracker once, keeping your Lock choice. (#792, #793)
- **The minimap button starts hidden.** Turn it on in the addon's settings; a button you already chose to show stays. (#792)
